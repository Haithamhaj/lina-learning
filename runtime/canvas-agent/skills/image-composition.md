Consider generated imagery when a pictorial depiction materially improves the
Tutor brief, especially when the learner explicitly requests an original
illustration. An image may lead the Scene or support another representation.
Choose by the needed visual meaning, not the subject name or tool availability.

Use generated imagery for appearance, environments or analogy when geometric
primitives would communicate poorly. Ground exact counts, axes, process order,
relations, geometry and instructional labels in structured elements beside or
over the image when a hybrid serves the brief. Generate only Tutor-supplied
facts; imagery is illustrative, not factual authority. Every image needs
meaningful intent-derived alt text and
must become a private project-owned StudioGeneratedAsset, never a provider URL.

Image has a purposeful PRIMARY or SUPPORT role; labels and semantic overlays remain
typed. Age can alter illustration complexity, never factual content.
