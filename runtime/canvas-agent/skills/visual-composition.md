# Visual composition
Teach through the representation itself, not a decorated frame around it. Let the
Tutor's goal and learner need set the visual direction; give the concept one
clear focal field in the Daily pane. Use intentional proportions and spacing,
coherent Arabic/RTL typography, palette and contrast, and short labels beside
the objects they explain. Keep distinct relationships legible (for example,
equilibrium, displacement and motion direction). Place feedback near what
changes. Motion must reveal a relationship or state; color supplements position,
shape or labels. Tutor owns the longer explanation.

Use the actual pane width before choosing sizes: keep labels readable, primary
controls at least 44px high and secondary targets at least 24px. Reflow controls
at narrower widths instead of shrinking the whole visual. Avoid nested
decorative frames and controls whose effect is visible only in their caption.

Final Scene and typed-block arguments use semantic layout/palette/motion roles.
For SCENE_2D, logical object positions span 0..100 across the renderer. Decimal fractions such as 0.5 are valid coordinates near the edge, not shorthand for 50. Keep distinct labels separated in the rendered 720x390 field; tool feedback reports clipped or overlapping label boxes so you can revise the composition or choose another suitable tool.
Custom source may use CSS/DOM/SVG within its sandbox. Neither path dictates one
layout for every concept. Inspect the 960px and 640px previews and the rendered
Daily pane; declared font sizes are not proof of readable screen sizes.

For each claimed control effect, review the causal change in the representation:
changing a caption alone does not establish a changed partition, arrangement,
position, quantity, or process. Check the visible objects and relationships against
the current parameters and canonical Manifest after interaction. Reject a visually
plausible interface whose control does not demonstrate its stated learning purpose.
Role labels and connecting lines must remain educationally valid after reordering.
