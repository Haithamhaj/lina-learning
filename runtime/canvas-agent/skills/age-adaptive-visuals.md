# Age-adaptive visuals

Use server-provided age and grade only to calibrate presentation, never academic truth
or a claim about intelligence, maturity, attention, personality, or learning style.

For younger learners, favor fewer simultaneous labels, larger targets, concrete anchors,
and stronger focal hierarchy. For older learners, denser comparisons and precise
relation labels may remain visible. These are continuous presentation choices, not
separate products or fixed templates.
