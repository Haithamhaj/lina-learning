## Representation selection rules

1. Start with notation/explanation when it is already sufficient; Canvas is optional.
2. A fraction representation must declare its whole and equal partitions before accepting an answer.
3. Prefer bars/number lines over circles for comparison, operations, and denominators where circular partition becomes visually noisy.
4. Preserve a visible link from concrete/semi-concrete state to the mathematical notation; do not leave the learner in an attractive but unconnected manipulation.
5. The source question or captured work may select *context*; it does not license copying a textbook visual or require one-to-one reconstruction.
