This skill is **development-only** for Codex/AI development agents. It is not
automatically available to Tutor or Canvas Specialist at runtime.

Read the [canonical guide](../../docs/LINA_EDUCATIONAL_VISUALS_GUIDE.md) and the
active task boundaries. Preserve accepted exact activities and Studio ownership.

1. Choose the instructional relationship first; Chat-only remains valid. Identify
   the existing renderer/semantic contract before adding any component.
2. Use spatial SVG for objects and meaningful directed relations, DOM for concise
   labels and longer focused details. No permanent stage paragraphs. Cycles must
   read as cycles; sequences must communicate direction. Preserve stable IDs.
3. Use only meaningful focus/de-emphasis/reveal/trace/focus transition. Movement
   or quantity transformations need instructional justification and invariants.
   Native CSS/SVG currently suffices; do not add Motion automatically.
4. Preserve RTL prose with explicit mixed-label bidi; do not mirror causal or
   mathematical direction. Use deliberate narrow layout, not indiscriminate scaling.
5. Verify keyboard/tap equivalence, visible focus, non-color distinction, text
   equivalent and reduced-motion information equivalence. Ordinal progress uses
   Stage N of M / المرحلة N من M, never ambiguous N/M.
6. Inspect asset provenance/rights and semantic handles; no raw executable model
   content or learner details in reusable content.
7. Inspect actual initial/intermediate/final screenshots and interrupted motion,
   wide/narrow, long labels and Arabic/English/mixed. Parsing/tests alone are not
   visual review. Compare [bad cycle cards](../../docs/reviews/STUDIO-VISUAL-PROCESS-01/VISUAL_CHECKPOINT.md)
   with [accepted spatial cycle and ordinal correction](../../docs/reviews/STUDIO-VISUAL-PROCESS-01/VISUAL_CHECKPOINT_2.md).

Stop for a Product Owner decision when scope, representation, dependency,
scientific grounding or authority conflicts cannot be resolved within approval.
Do not ask again for work already explicitly authorized. Report exactly what
mock, visual, live-model and learning-benefit evidence does and does not prove.
