# Educational visuals policy

## Purpose

A visual representation should exist because it improves learning, not because Lina has a Canvas.

Useful visual goals include:

- orientation;
- explanation;
- comparison;
- grouping;
- sequencing;
- spatial understanding;
- process understanding;
- interaction;
- feedback;
- a checkable learning objective.

## Teaching and representation

Keep these concepts separate:

- Teaching Mode — broad interaction posture.
- Teaching Strategy — organization of the learning move.
- Teaching Method — instructional method or representation approach.
- Surface — Chat, Canvas, source, voice, or another delivery surface.

Canvas is a surface, not a teaching method and not a separate teaching authority.

## When to use visuals

A visual is strong when:

- language is becoming repetitive or overly long;
- structure or spatial relation carries the meaning;
- comparison is easier to see than describe;
- the learner explicitly needs to see the idea;
- meaningful manipulation or grouping supports understanding;
- a process or relation is clearer as a sequence or diagram.

Stay in Chat when the explanation is short, the visual would be decorative, source meaning is uncertain, or no accurate capability fits.

## Full-Power representation

Current approved capabilities include typed React/SVG renderers, Motion, JSXGraph, Konva, MathLive, reusable visual artifacts, custom sandboxed visual packages, and project-owned generated assets.

The implementation technology should follow the educational representation need.

## Exact truth

Math and scientific relationships must come from typed inputs, trusted grounded information, or deterministic tools.

A renderer or generated visual must not invent missing values or authoritative scientific text.

## Personalization

Visual personalization is optional.

Safe Personal Facts may change presentation flavor when naturally useful, but they must not change educational truth, difficulty, teaching method, or learner-state interpretation.

JEV may select among an already filtered Personal Fact catalogue for a specific visual brief.

## Interaction

Meaningful learner actions need stable semantic identity.

Raw clicks and pointer coordinates are not automatically educational meaning.

## Generated educational images

Generated educational images are a separate planned capability.

Initial direction is Science-first for shape, structure, process, and scene needs, with Canvas delivery and instructional annotations when useful.

Generated pixels are illustrative. Exact labels and relationships must come from grounded information.
